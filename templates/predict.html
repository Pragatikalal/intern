Demand Prediction

Trains four candidate regressors (Linear, Ridge, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting) on weekday/month cycles, 1-day and 7-day lags and the 7-day rolling mean, tests them on the most recent 20% of days, keeps the best by RMSE, then forecasts forward. Requires at least 45 days of data.
